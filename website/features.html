Every Feature, Explained

Everything you need to take control of your finances in one beautiful, private app.

Automation

SMS Auto-Import

Never manually enter a transaction again. Mudra reads bank SMS and RCS notifications from 50+ Indian banks — HDFC, SBI, ICICI, Axis, Kotak, Paytm, PhonePe, GPay — plus international banks in 50+ countries.

  • Dual-SIM and RCS support (Google Messages, Samsung Messages)
  • Smart category matching that learns your patterns
  • 100% local processing — nothing sent to any server
Planning

Smart Budgets

4-step guided budget creation with category allocation, spending pace tracking, and intelligent alerts. Supports category-wise, tag-wise, day-wise, festival, and travel budget types.

  • Alerts at 80% and 100% with tone-aware messaging
  • Daily, weekly, monthly, yearly recurrence
  • Under-budget spending streaks with achievements
Insights

Analytics & Reports

Spending personality, financial health score, monthly comparisons, category trends. Morning Money Minute delivers a personalized insight every day. Export to Excel and PDF.

  • Morning insight: yesterday's spend vs your 30-day average
  • Weekly recap nudge with top spending category
  • Money leak detection and savings opportunities
Privacy

Bank-Level Security

AES-256 field-level encryption for sensitive data. Biometric lock, PIN protection, guest mode. Zero cloud, zero servers, zero data collection. We literally can't see your data.

  • Encryption key stored in Android Keystore / iOS Keychain
  • Graceful degradation — app works even if encryption fails
  • Guest mode hides all amounts from prying eyes
