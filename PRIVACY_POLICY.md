# Privacy Policy for Mudra Manager

**Last Updated: January 2025**

## Introduction
Mudra Manager ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

## Data Collection
**We DO NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.**

### Local Data Storage
- All financial data is stored locally on your device
- Transaction records, accounts, budgets, and categories remain on your device
- No cloud synchronization or external data transmission

### SMS Permissions (Android Only)
- SMS permission is used ONLY to read transaction messages from banks/wallets
- SMS data is processed locally on your device
- We do NOT upload, share, or transmit SMS content to any server
- You can disable SMS import anytime in settings

### Biometric Authentication (Optional)
- Used only for local device authentication
- Biometric data never leaves your device
- Managed by your device's secure enclave

### Notifications
- Local notifications for reminders and alerts
- No data sent to external notification services

## Data We DO NOT Collect
- ❌ Personal identification information
- ❌ Financial account credentials
- ❌ Location data
- ❌ Device identifiers
- ❌ Usage analytics
- ❌ Crash reports

## Third-Party Services
We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## Data Backup
- Backup/restore feature stores data locally on your device
- You control where backups are saved
- No automatic cloud uploads

## Children's Privacy
Our app does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Data Security
- All data stored using device encryption
- Optional biometric/PIN protection
- No network transmission of financial data

## Your Rights
You have complete control over your data:
- Export your data anytime
- Delete all data from settings
- Uninstall app to remove all data

## Changes to Privacy Policy
We may update this policy. Changes will be posted in the app.

## Contact Us
For privacy concerns:
- Email: support@mudramanager.app
- Website: mudramanager.app

## Compliance
This app complies with:
- Google Play Store policies
- Indian data protection regulations
- GDPR principles (data minimization)

---

**Summary: Mudra Manager is a privacy-first app. All your financial data stays on your device. We don't collect, store, or share your personal information.**
