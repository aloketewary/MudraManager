FAQ

Is my financial data secure?

All data stored locally with AES-256 field-level encryption. Keys in Android Keystore / iOS Keychain. Zero servers.

How does SMS auto-import work?

Reads bank SMS/RCS locally using 50+ bank-specific parsers. All on-device. Nothing sent anywhere.

Can I use the app offline?

100% offline. Isar local database, zero network dependency.

How do I backup my data?

Settings → Backup & Restore → Export to encrypted backup, Excel, or PDF.

Is the app free?

Core features free forever. Pro unlocks unlimited accounts and advanced analytics.

What languages are supported?

Full EN, HI, BN. Partial ES, PT, ID. Stubs for all 22 scheduled Indian languages.

How do I delete all data?

Settings → Clear All Data, or uninstall. No servers = nothing to delete on our end.
